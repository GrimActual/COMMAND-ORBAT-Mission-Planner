# COMMAND helicopter controls — experimental

Use **COMMAND 1.1.0** with **bridge 0.15.0**. Install the aircraft’s required addons on the game/server and participating clients. Use one bridge copy and pair explicitly. Available exact aircraft profiles are listed in the bundled support catalog; pending, absent or unindexed models remain unavailable. A family name does not establish support for every variant.

1. Open **Mission & deployment**. Choose an available aircraft in **Helicopter test mission**, then **Open helicopter test mission**. The existing Huey and Mi-8MT sample buttons also remain available. Review the import, then apply it. Importing changes the plan only; Undo restores the previous plan.
2. Use **Fit ORBAT** if the markers are off-screen. Inspect or move the departure to a clear, flat area with room for the airframe and rotors. Review deployment, send it and approve in Reforger. Verify the actual occupants before flight.
3. Refresh flight targets and choose the aircraft. For a four-passenger insertion, select the passenger **TEAM**, not **PILOT**. For a verified aircraft-plus-pilot mission, enable **Pilot only — no passengers**; no passenger team is required. Do not use that mode with extra occupants.
4. Choose travel speed, add map waypoints and mark a clear, flat final LZ. Valid world coordinates and up to **16 intermediate points** apply. There is no artificial flight-path distance minimum, maximum or minimum waypoint spacing. Very close points may be reached without visible travel.
5. Leave waypoint heights blank for automatic cruise. Optional values are **minimum metres above ground**. The highest entered minimum applies to the entire cruise; the aircraft may fly higher. The controller samples route terrain and raises cruise height when its preflight corridor check detects collision geometry. This does not guarantee tree clearance or dynamic obstacle avoidance. COMMAND does not relocate your LZ automatically; takeoff and landing columns must be clear.
6. Choose **Land and unload**, **Fly route then hover**, or **Loop route**. Review the complete route, send it and approve flight separately in Reforger. Only one assisted flight can run at a time. The paired session must remain authorized for a patrol.

**Land / stop patrol** follows the reviewed remaining route to the approved LZ and retains passengers. A four-passenger landing completes only after the designated team exits and the pilot stays aboard. Pilot-only completion waits for sustained grounded conditions and stopped rotors; **Stopping rotors** is still in progress. Do not disconnect to stop airborne control.

Keep observers outside during assistance. Human takeover and two-client multiplayer acceptance remain pending. Short isolated checks apply only to the exact tested configuration and source revision; they do not establish every aircraft variant, weapon operation, long flight or heavy-load performance. Check the catalog’s current status and limitations. Longer routes, populated-server behavior and human lifecycle checks remain operator acceptance tasks. The historical walking-to-vehicle boarding timeout is unrelated and is not claimed fixed.
